About Our Department

Excellence in Computer Science Education and Research

Pioneering Computer Science Education

The Department of Computer Science at Mankar College was established in 2005 with a vision to provide quality education in the field of computer science and information technology. Over the years, we have evolved into a center of excellence, nurturing talent and fostering innovation.

Our department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, a comprehensive library, and experienced faculty members dedicated to providing students with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills.
