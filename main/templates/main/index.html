Pioneering Computer Science Education

The Department of Computer Science at Mankar College was established in 2005 with a vision to provide quality education in the field of computer science and information technology. Over the years, we have evolved into a center of excellence, nurturing talent and fostering innovation.

Our department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, a comprehensive library, and experienced faculty members dedicated to providing students with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills.

Message From HOD

Words of Wisdom and Guidance

Dr. Amitava Bandhupadhyay

Head of Department

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science at Mankar College. Our mission is to provide a transformative educational experience that prepares students to be leaders in the rapidly evolving field of technology.

In today's digital era, computer science education is not just about learning programming languages or algorithms; it's about developing problem-solving skills, critical thinking, and a mindset of continuous learning. Our department strives to create an environment where students can explore, innovate, and grow both academically and personally.

We have designed our curriculum to blend theoretical foundations with practical applications, ensuring our graduates are well-equipped to meet the demands of the industry or pursue higher education. Our faculty members are dedicated educators and active researchers who bring their expertise and passion into the classroom.

I invite you to explore the opportunities our department offers and join us in our journey of academic excellence and technological advancement.
